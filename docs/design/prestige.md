# Guild Prestige System

The former level-200 and unbounded-prestige proposal in this location is superseded.

The approved Chapter 2 contract is maintained in:

- `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-30-chapter-2-prestige-gold-design.md`
- REQ-049, REQ-050, REQ-051, REQ-054, REQ-056, REQ-092, and REQ-093

The replacement design uses a level-100 current-run reset, a lifetime maximum of three prestiges, raw-gold costs of 10,000/20,000/30,000, one permanent perk plus one permanent home per prestige, strict war/capacity eligibility, and no Elo reset. It ships disabled until enabled by the operator.
